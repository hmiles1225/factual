      What this is

      fACTUAL reads the public news feeds of a fixed list of outlets every hour. It groups articles about the same event by what they say, not by matching words. A story needs at least two outlets. Opinion pieces are kept separate from news.

      Headlines here are the outlets’ own words, shown in italic and named. We link to every article and never republish article text.

      The method, the outlet list and the code are public: github.com/hmiles1225/factual.

      For publishers

      Our scanner identifies itself as fACTUAL-scan and reads each feed once an hour. To ask a question or request a change, open an issue on GitHub.
