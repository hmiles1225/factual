Today

Today, in … stories.

Only stories that both left- and right-leaning outlets are covering. What they agree on first, then where they differ, and what holds up.

What this is

fACTUAL reads the public news feeds of a fixed list of outlets every hour and groups articles about the same event by what they say. Today shows U.S. politics and U.S. policy abroad, plus environment, science and health, topics that get politicized. A story appears only when outlets on both the left and the right are covering it. Every story that appears is kept in the archive.

Claims in Today’s stories are checked by AI against primary sources. Every quote shown was found on its source page by our code, a Doesn’t hold up needs two different AI models to agree, and no person reviews a check before it appears.

Headlines are the outlets’ own words, shown in italic and named. To write each summary, the scanner reads the opening paragraphs of each article; that text is used once and never stored or republished. We link to every article. The method, the outlet list and the code are public: github.com/hmiles1225/factual.

For publishers

Our scanner identifies itself as fACTUAL-scan and reads each feed once an hour. To ask a question or request a change, open an issue on GitHub.
