The political stories the most outlets are covering. What they agree on first, then what holds up.

    What this is

    fACTUAL reads the public news feeds of a fixed list of outlets every hour and groups articles about the same event by what they say. Today shows U.S. politics and U.S. policy abroad.

    Claims in Today’s stories are checked by AI against primary sources. Every quote shown was found on its source page by our code, a Doesn’t hold up needs two different AI models to agree, and no person reviews a check before it appears.

    Headlines are the outlets’ own words, shown in italic and named. We link to every article and never republish article text. The method, the outlet list and the code are public: github.com/hmiles1225/factual.

    For publishers

    Our scanner identifies itself as fACTUAL-scan and reads each feed once an hour. To ask a question or request a change, open an issue on GitHub.
