## Why each verdict, and its source

### p01L · Doesn't hold up
> Measles cases in the U.S. are at an all-time high.

2025 had 2,065 confirmed cases, the most since 1992 (2,126). 1958 alone had 763,094 reported cases.
- CDC measles case count, Dec 31 2025 (via coverage) *(secondary)*
- 1958 count: fACTUAL verified facts, 2026-10-17 *(secondary)*

### p01R · Doesn't hold up
> Violent crime in America is at an all-time high.

FBI 2025 estimates: violent crime fell 9.3% from 2024; the murder rate, 4.1 per 100,000, tied 1955 and 1956 as the lowest.
- FBI 2025 crime data (via HSToday) *(secondary)*: https://www.hstoday.us/subject-matter-areas/law-enforcement-and-public-safety/fbi-releases-2025-crime-data-showing-sharp-drop-in-violent-offenses/

### p02L · Doesn't hold up
> CEOs now make 1,000 times what their typical worker makes.

EPI's measure puts the ratio at 290 to 1 for 2023 (realized pay, 350 largest firms); its peak was 344 to 1 in 2000.
- Economic Policy Institute CEO pay report, 2024 (via coverage) *(secondary)*

### p02R · Doesn't hold up
> Inflation under Biden was the worst in American history.

CPI inflation peaked at 9.0% year over year in June 2022 (seasonally adjusted; 9.1% unadjusted). It reached 14.6% in March 1980.
- BLS CPI-U via FRED, series CPIAUCSL: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL

### p03L · Doesn't hold up
> Unemployment is at a 50-year low.

Unemployment was 4.2% in September 2026 and has been 4.1 to 4.3% since March 2026.
- BLS Employment Situation, Sept 2026 (released Oct 2 2026): https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_10022026.htm

### p03R · Doesn't hold up
> Illegal border crossings are at record highs.

Southwest border apprehensions ran about 6,000 to 10,000 a month in fiscal 2026 (6,070 in January 2026), far below the fiscal 2022-23 peak.
- CBP monthly figures (via House Homeland Security border brief) *(secondary)*: https://homeland.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Oct-FY26-Border-Brief.pdf

### p04R · Doesn't hold up
> Only about half a percent of Americans are transgender.

0.5% was the Williams Institute's 2022 estimate. Its August 2025 update is 0.8% of adults and 3.3% of ages 13 to 17.
- Williams Institute, UCLA, Aug 2025 (fACTUAL verified facts, 2026-10-17) *(secondary)*

### p04L · Doesn't hold up
> The U.S. uninsured rate is at an all-time low.

8.3% of people were uninsured in 2025, not significantly different from 8.2% in 2024, so 2025 is not a record low.
- CDC NCHS, NHIS early release 2025 (May 28 2026): https://cdc.gov/nchs/pressroom/releases/20260528.html

### p05L · Doesn't hold up
> Most of the 2017 Trump tax cut went to the top 1% in its first year.

The Tax Policy Center estimated the top 1% received about 20.5% of the 2018 tax cut.
- Tax Policy Center via FactCheck.org *(secondary)*: https://www.factcheck.org/2018/01/democrats-misleading-tax-line
- Tax Policy Center, Three numbers to know about the TCJA in 2018: https://www.taxpolicycenter.org/taxvox/three-numbers-know-about-tcja-2018

### p05R · Doesn't hold up
> The MMR vaccine causes autism.

A Danish cohort of 657,461 children found no increased risk (HR 0.93, 95% CI 0.85 to 1.02). Cochrane 2020 reached the same conclusion.
- Hviid et al., Annals of Internal Medicine 2019, doi 10.7326/M18-2101: https://doi.org/10.7326/M18-2101

### p06L · Doesn't hold up
> Most federal spending goes to the military.

National defense was $916 billion in fiscal 2025, about 13% of federal spending (OMB).
- Peter G. Peterson Foundation, citing OMB *(secondary)*: https://www.pgpf.org/budget-basics/budget-explainer-national-defense

### p06R · Doesn't hold up
> Millions of noncitizens voted in the 2020 election.

Georgia's 2024 citizenship audit found 20 noncitizens among 8.2 million registrants; 9 had ever voted. No audit has found numbers near millions.
- AP via ABC News, Georgia citizenship audit, Oct 2024 *(secondary)*: https://abcnews.go.com/US/wireStory/georgia-citizenship-audit-finds-noncitizens-voter-rolls-115073753

### p07L · Holds up
> The U.S. had more measles cases in 2025 than in any year since 1992.

2,065 confirmed cases in 2025; 1992 had 2,126.
- CDC measles case count, Dec 31 2025 (via coverage) *(secondary)*

### p07R · Missing context (corrected 2026-10-17, was Holds up)
> Interest on the federal debt passed $1 trillion in fiscal 2025.

True on two official measures, not on a third. CBO's year-end review says net interest on the public debt "for the first time, surpassed $1 trillion"; GAO says interest on the debt was $1.2 trillion. CBO's standard budget measure, net outlays for interest, was $970 billion in 2025. Which measure is meant changes the answer.
- CBO Monthly Budget Review: Summary for Fiscal Year 2025: https://cbo.gov/publication/61307
- CBO Budget and Economic Outlook: 2026 to 2036 (Feb 2026): https://www.cbo.gov/publication/62105
- GAO-26-107908: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-107908
- **Why it changed:** the original answer rested on one CBO line. The pilot checker found CBO's $970 billion figure, and code verified the quote on cbo.gov.

### p12L · Missing context

### p13L · Missing context

### p14L · Missing context
> Fossil fuels get $7 trillion a year in subsidies.

True as the IMF's 2022 estimate. Missing: 82% is implicit (unpriced pollution and climate costs); direct subsidies were $1.3 trillion.
- IMF working paper, Black, Parry and Vernon, Aug 2023 (via coverage) *(secondary)*

### p14R · Missing context

### p15L · Missing context

### p15R · Missing context

- IRS data via Tax Foundation and fact-check coverage *(secondary)*

### p17L · Unproven
> Medicare for All would save the country money.

A forecast. Estimates disagree: a Lancet study projected about $450 billion a year in savings; Urban Institute, RAND and Mercatus estimates project higher spending.
- KFF Health News and PolitiFact, 2020 *(secondary)*: https://kffhealthnews.org/news/bernie-sanders-embraces-a-new-study-that-lowers-medicare-for-alls-price-tag-but-skepticism-abounds/

### p17R · Unproven
> A $15 minimum wage would cost more than a million jobs.

A forecast. CBO's 2021 estimate averaged 1.4 million fewer jobs, median 1 million, with a range from about zero to 2.7 million or more.
- FactCheck.org on CBO, Feb 2021 *(secondary)*: https://www.factcheck.org/2021/01/paul-distorts-cbos-estimate-on-impact-of-15-minimum-wage/

### p18L · Unproven
> Voter ID laws reduce turnout among minority voters.

Mixed. GAO (2014) found turnout fell 1.9 to 3.2 points more in Kansas and Tennessee after ID laws. Cantoni and Pons (QJE 2021, 2008-2018 national panel) found no negative effect overall or by race.
- GAO-14-634: https://www.gao.gov/products/GAO-14-634
- Cantoni and Pons, NBER w25522: https://www.nber.org/papers/w25522

### p18R · Unproven
> Fluoride in U.S. tap water lowers children's IQ.

NTP (2024) found an association with lower IQ above 1.5 mg/L. The U.S. target is 0.7 mg/L, where the data were insufficient.
- National Toxicology Program, 2024 (fACTUAL verified facts, 2026-10-17) *(secondary)*

### p19L · Unproven
> Glyphosate, the weedkiller in Roundup, causes cancer.

Mixed. IARC (2015) classed it as probably carcinogenic; EFSA and EPA concluded it is not likely carcinogenic; EPA withdrew its interim decision in 2022 and planned a new review in 2026.
- Farm Progress on IARC, EFSA and EPA *(secondary)*: https://www.farmprogress.com/cotton/epa-classifies-glyphosate-as-not-likely-carcinogenic-then-pulls-report

### p19R · Unproven
> Masks did nothing to stop COVID.

Mixed. Cochrane (2023) found community masking probably makes little or no difference, with a wide confidence interval and mostly pre-COVID trials; Cochrane later said the review was widely misinterpreted.
- Cochrane review, Jefferson et al. 2023: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9885521/
- Cochrane statement, March 2023: https://www.cochrane.org/news/statement-physical-interventions-interrupt-or-reduce-spread-respiratory-viruses-review
